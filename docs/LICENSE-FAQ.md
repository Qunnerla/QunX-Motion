# License FAQ

Short answers about the [QunX Motion License](../LICENSE). If this page and `LICENSE` disagree, `LICENSE` wins; the current terms are the ones in this repository.

**Can I use QunX Motion for free?**
Yes, for personal and client work, paid jobs included.

**Do the clips I make belong to me? Do they need a QunX credit?**
Yes, they are yours, and no credit is needed in the clips. You can sell clips, use them in ads and deliver them to clients.

**Can I change the skill?**
Yes. Fork it, edit it, add templates, presets or workflows, translate it, rewrite parts of it for your own workflow.

**Can I share my changed version?**
Yes, for free, as long as you keep the `LICENSE` file, the attribution block at the top of `SKILL.md` (original project, original creator QunX, canonical source, license ID) and say what you changed. See [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md).

**Can I remove the credit or say I made it?**
No. You can add your name as the author of *your changes*, never as the original creator.

**Can I sell the skill, or a changed version, or put it in a paid course or bundle?**
No, not without written permission from QunX. This is about the skill only, not the clips you make with it.

**Is this open source?**
No. It is free to use and modify, with attribution and no-resale conditions.

**Does the "integrity" section stop people from editing the files?**
No. It asks AI assistants that load the skill to keep the attribution. It is not DRM and cannot stop anyone editing the files. The license conditions apply either way.

**Which version of the license applies?**
The one published here, at https://github.com/Qunnerla/QunX-Motion. QunX may update it; older copies don't override it.
